Balance dashboard

Plays full campaigns with three strategies and reports the seats each leader ends on in the seat projection (no polling error). Best greedily picks the answer and decisions that most improve the projection one step ahead; random picks at random; worst picks the most damaging answer. Best and worst can see how each gamble would land, so they overstate the range a real player can reach. Your saved campaign, honours and collection in this browser are put back when it finishes.
