Australia 2028 · Campaign tier list

Who to play

All 65 standard Campaign Mode leaders graded A to F, from 1,200 simulated campaigns (excluding the five craft-only Vault Legends). A is the strongest pick and the easiest to win with; F is the hardest.

About these grades. Grades measure how hard each leader is to win with in this game’s current scenario, not a judgement of the people themselves.

How the grades were worked out

Every standard leader played at least 16 complete campaigns on Standard difficulty from the As it stands opening: 12 with sub-optimal selections (choices made at random), 3 with optimal selections (always the option that looked best one step ahead), and 1 always taking the worst. The closest calls and rebalanced leaders (Albanese, Andrews, Conroy, Hanson, Joyce, Latham, Morrison, Roberts, Rudd and Stephen Smith) played 24 or 48 sub-optimal campaigns to settle their grades. Each campaign was scored by the tier it reached for that leader’s own party, so a Greens leader winning six seats counts the same as a Labor leader winning a landslide: Tier 1 scores 3, Tier 2 scores 2, Tier 3 scores 1, a defeat 0, and losing the leadership spill or the by-election before the campaign starts −0.5.

A leader’s strength is 65% the sub-optimal games, which are closest to a first-time player, and 35% the optimal games, the ceiling with good decisions. The grades cut that score into six bands. Results are the seat projection at the end of the campaign, without the extra polling error of election night, so the real count will sometimes land a tier higher or lower.

Typical result is the most common outcome with sub-optimal selections. Optimal selections is the best tier reached with optimal selections. Wins is how often sub-optimal selections reached one of the top two tiers. Knocked out is how often the leader never reached the campaign.
