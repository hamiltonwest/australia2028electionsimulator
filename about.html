About this page

An independent election simulator built from a personal Excel model. It is not affiliated with any political party, pollster, broadcaster or the Australian Electoral Commission. XYZ News and its reporters are fictional, and the election-night articles are simulated text: they contain no real quotes.

Data sources

  • Australian Electoral Commission: 2025 House and Senate results, two-candidate-preferred flows by party, electoral boundaries (March 2025).
  • Published national opinion polls to November 2026, and the DemosAU seat-by-seat MRP (August–September 2026).
  • Australian Bureau of Statistics 2021 Census and the ABS Australian coastline; ATO HELP-debt figures by electorate.
  • Party leaders, dates and changes since 2025: Wikipedia and news reports, as cited in the build notes.
