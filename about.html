About the simulator

Who made it, where the numbers come from, and the fine print.

An independent, non-commercial election simulator. It is not affiliated with any political party, pollster, broadcaster, the Parliament of Australia or the Australian Electoral Commission. XYZ News and its reporters are fictional, every result is a simulation rather than a prediction, and the news stories contain no real quotes.

Sources

  • Results and boundaries: Australian Electoral Commission, 2025 House and Senate results, preference flows and electoral boundaries.
  • Polls: published national opinion polls to November 2026 (listed under Polls and data) and the DemosAU seat-by-seat MRP poll, August–September 2026.
  • Seat profiles: ABS 2021 Census and ATO HELP-debt statistics by electorate.
  • Leaders, party changes and factions: Wikipedia (including its Labor Left, Labor Right, Moderates, National Right and Centre Right pages) and news reports, checked November 2026.

Credits

  • Members and Senators (and former Members Sussan Ley, Bill Shorten, Josh Frydenberg, Warren Truss, Adam Bandt, Cathy McGowan and Kerryn Phelps): official portraits, © Commonwealth of Australia, AUSPIC / Department of Parliamentary Services, from the Parliament of Australia website under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, shown unaltered.
  • Malcolm Turnbull and Robbie Katter: photos from Wikimedia Commons, with the background, lighting and framing edited to match.
  • Party logos: the Labor, Liberal, Centre Alliance and Community Strong Australia logos are Wikipedia placeholder files, used only to identify the parties.
  • House of Representatives chamber: photo from Wikimedia Commons (File:Australian House of Representatives - Parliament of Australia.jpg).
  • Placeholders: anyone else is shown with an illustrated placeholder portrait.
