About the simulator

What this project is, where the numbers come from, how your data is stored, and full credits for every portrait and trading card.

Overview Data sources Storage and privacy Image licences Full photo credits

The Australia 2028 Election Simulator is an independent, non-commercial interactive simulation of Australia’s next federal election. Built on official 2025 Australian Electoral Commission (AEC) electorate results, real federal boundaries, published polling and 2021 Census seat profiles, it lets you test election scenarios in two distinct modes.

Simulation engine

The Sandbox

Run thousands of seat-by-seat simulations across all 150 House of Representatives divisions and the Senate (half-Senate or double dissolution). Adjust national and state swings, party leaders, preference flows, campaign events, demographic movements and local seat factors, then replay results night live or inspect the pendulum, path to 76 and Senate count.

Interactive game

Campaign Mode

Lead Labor, the Coalition (Liberals and Nationals), One Nation, the Greens, the teal independents or Katter’s Australian Party across a 25-turn, five-week campaign. Choose from 70 playable leaders — 65 Series One leaders plus five craft-only Vault Legends — navigating leadership spills, by-elections, doorstop interviews, hung-parliament deals, Honours, Daily Challenges and the Collection Binder.

Independence and fiction notice: This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any political party, candidate, pollster, broadcaster, the Parliament of Australia or the Australian Electoral Commission. XYZ News, its reporters and its studio guests are entirely fictional. Every seat tally, probability and election-night report is a simulation rather than a prediction, and none of the news stories, answers or campaign choices represent real quotes or positions of real people.

Data and methodology sources

  • Results, preference flows and boundaries: Australian Electoral Commission (AEC) 2025 federal election House of Representatives and Senate results, distribution-of-preference flows and current electoral division boundaries.
  • Opinion polls and MRP: Published national first-preference polls through to November 2026 (listed in full under Polls and data) and the DemosAU seat-by-seat MRP poll (August–September 2026).
  • Electorate profiles: Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) 2021 Census of Population and Housing (35 electorate-level demographic measures) and Australian Taxation Office (ATO) HELP-debt statistics by electorate.
  • Parliamentary records, party changes and factions: Parliamentary handbooks, Wikipedia (including its Labor Left, Labor Right, Liberal Moderates, National Right and Centre Right faction articles) and Australian news reporting, checked November 2026.
  • Model design and leader balance: Read How it works for full details on polling error modelling, the 2025 back-test and known limitations, and the Tier list for how the 65 standard Campaign Mode leaders are graded across simulated runs.

Storage and privacy

Everything in the simulator runs locally in your browser. No account is required, and your settings or game progress are never sent to a remote server.

Your active Campaign Mode run, earned Honours, Hall of Fame history, Daily Challenge bests, leader-only answers, Card Dust, foil upgrades and unlocked trading cards are saved only in your browser’s localStorage. You can export a backup file or profile code from the Hall of Fame to move your progress between devices, or wipe all saved progress at any time using the ResetProfile code.
